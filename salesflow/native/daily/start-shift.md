# Salesflow Instruction: Start Your Shift

- **Roles:** All operators
- **Work Priority:** Appointments → Live Calls → Assigned Tasks → Call Queue
- **Last logic review:** 2026-08-23
- **Instruction type:** Native
- **UI verification:** Draft — client verification required

## Outcome

You are available for the correct work, prepared for scheduled commitments, and aware of your due responsibilities.

## Steps

1. Sign in to the correct Salesflow client account.
2. Confirm your name and the client account shown in the workspace.
3. Set your availability to the client-approved working state.
4. Confirm your headset, microphone, speaker, and browser calling permission.
5. Open **Calendars → Appointment List View**.
6. Filter to your appointments for today and review the next preparation window.
7. Open **Contacts → Tasks**.
8. Filter to tasks assigned to you that are due or overdue.
9. Confirm the available Sales Officer, Support, Onboarding, and management coverage for handoffs.
10. If no appointment, Live Call, or due Assigned Task requires attention, open **Conversations → Manual Actions** and begin the Call Queue.

## Completion standard

- You are in the correct client account.
- Your availability is accurate.
- Calling works.
- You know your next appointment and due task.
- You know where live handoffs can be sent.

## Common mistakes

- Working in the wrong client account.
- Appearing available while unable to accept calls.
- Opening the Call Queue without checking appointments and due tasks.
- Beginning work without knowing who can accept a transfer.

# Salesflow Instruction: Add a Meaningful Note

- **Roles:** All users
- **Last logic review:** 2026-08-23
- **Instruction type:** Native
- **UI verification:** Draft — client verification required

## Outcome

Important internal context is preserved without burying the record in automated-event noise.

## Use a note for

- motivation and service goals;
- material objections;
- preferences and restrictions;
- decision-makers;
- unusual circumstances;
- important commitments;
- payment confirmation without sensitive payment data;
- context needed by another role.

## Where to go

Open the correct **Contact** or **Opportunity**, then open the **Notes** panel.

## Steps

1. Confirm you are on the correct contact or opportunity.
2. Open **Notes**.
3. Click **Add Note**.
4. Add a short searchable title.
5. Write the facts, customer meaning, and any commitment clearly.
6. Add the approved color or attachment only when useful.
7. Do not include unnecessary sensitive data.
8. Save and confirm the note appears on the correct record.

## Do not use a note for

- a due action;
- an exact-time callback;
- a pipeline stage;
- every automated SMS, email, workflow, or call attempt;
- passwords, complete card data, banking credentials, Social Security numbers, or unnecessary protected information.

## Note format

**Context:** What matters.  
**Customer position:** What they said or decided.  
**Commitment:** What was promised, by whom, and when.  
**Next owner:** Only when responsibility has been accepted.

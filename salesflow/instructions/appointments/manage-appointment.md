# Salesflow Instruction: Prepare, Complete, and Update an Appointment

- **Roles:** Booking Agent, Sales Officer, Onboarding Coordinator, Support Representative
- **Work Priority:** Appointments
- **Last logic review:** 2026-08-23
- **Salesflow dependency:** Core capability
- **UI verification:** Draft — client verification required

## Outcome

The scheduled commitment begins on time, advances the customer, and ends with an accurate appointment status and next system action.

## Where to go

**Salesflow → Calendars → Appointment List View**

## Click-by-click walkthrough

1. Filter Appointment List View to your approved list and time period.
2. Open the appointment before its preparation window.
3. Verify the contact, time, purpose, owner, meeting method, and current status.
4. Review the opportunity stage, Call Brief, due tasks, and meaningful notes.
5. Begin the appointment on time.
6. Move the customer to the strongest valid next step.
7. After the appointment, use the appointment row **Status** control or open the row menu.
8. Select the truthful client-approved status, such as Confirmed, Showed, No Show, Cancelled, or Invalid.
9. Record the sales or service outcome separately when the appointment status does not express it.
10. Update the opportunity and create the correct next appointment, task, onboarding handoff, or Recovery.

## Completion standard

The appointment status, customer outcome, opportunity, and next action must agree.

## If something goes wrong

- **No Show:** set the accurate status and enter Appointment Recovery immediately.
- **Rescheduled:** create or confirm the replacement appointment and ensure the old reminders will not conflict.
- **Cancelled:** record the reason and choose the correct Recovery or valid exit.
- **Customer ready earlier:** use Expedited movement and resolve the appointment if it becomes obsolete.

## Common mistakes

- Marking Showed before the appointment occurs.
- Treating a No Show as an ordinary Call Queue attempt.
- Updating the appointment but not the opportunity.
- Leaving reminders active for an obsolete appointment.

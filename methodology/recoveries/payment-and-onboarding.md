# Payment and Onboarding Recovery

## Payment Recovery

Use when payment is declined, abandoned, incomplete, disputed, overdue, or inconsistent with the customer record.

The recovery must protect sensitive information, preserve the commitment, assign an owner, and provide a clear next step without storing prohibited payment data in notes or tasks.

## Onboarding Recovery

Use when a committed customer has incomplete agreements, documents, registration, setup, provisioning, orientation, or activation.

The Onboarding Coordinator owns the missing requirement and due time. If another department is required, use a documented and accepted handoff.

## Success

Payment is correctly reflected, onboarding is complete, the customer is activated, and Support receives the required context.

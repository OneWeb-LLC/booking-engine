# Management System and KPIs

## Accountability split

### Agents own

- availability;
- punctual appointments;
- live-call acceptance;
- task completion;
- Call Queue execution;
- dispositions;
- documentation quality.

### Campaign managers own

- qualified work supply;
- queue depth;
- workflow integrity;
- cadence and retry logic;
- suppression;
- campaign performance;
- source attribution.

### Team managers own

- staffing and coverage;
- coaching;
- quality assurance;
- overdue commitments;
- escalation resolution.

## Queue health

| State | Definition | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy | More than one productive day | Monitor |
| Low | Less than four productive hours | Warn campaign manager |
| Critical | Less than one productive hour | Add eligible recovery/reactivation inventory |
| Empty | No eligible records | Escalate immediately |

## Core metrics

### Responsiveness

- speed-to-lead;
- appointment on-time rate;
- inbound answer rate;
- Live Call acceptance rate;
- Zero Friction connect and transfer rates.

### Execution

- assigned-task completion rate;
- overdue assigned tasks;
- Call Queue attempts;
- queue depth and estimated productive hours;
- disposition compliance;
- documentation compliance.

### Funnel

- contact rate;
- meaningful-conversation rate;
- booking rate;
- held-appointment rate;
- recovery rate;
- reactivation rate;
- conversion and collected revenue by source, campaign, and agent.

### Path performance

- Standard Path completion rate;
- Expedited Path entry, close, Buy Now, and warm-transfer rates;
- Recovery entry and restoration rate;
- time and touches from Capture to Close by path;
- conversion rate by configured Convert milestone;
- duplicate or obsolete work prevented;
- invalid step-skip exceptions.

## Management principle

Activity is diagnostic, not the final outcome. The system should connect effort to customer movement and revenue.

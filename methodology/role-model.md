# Roles and Ownership

Roles are functions, not mandatory headcount. One person may perform several roles in a small business; larger businesses may separate them by team.

## Campaign Manager

**Mission:** Generate qualified demand and maintain sufficient eligible work.

Owns campaigns, attribution, workflow enrollment, queue supply, cadence, suppression, and conversion visibility.

## Booking Agent

**Mission:** Convert inbound interest into a qualified live conversation, appointment, or defined next action.

Owns inbound response, Zero Friction, minimum intake, qualification, routing, warm transfers, booking, missed-call recovery, activation recovery, and booking recovery.

The Booking Agent is the front-line operator and momentum manager of the Booking Engine—not merely a scheduler. A client may authorize the Booking Agent to complete defined Expedited closes. Anything outside that authority must be handed to the appropriate Sales Officer.

## Sales Officer

**Mission:** Convert qualified interest into a customer commitment.

Owns consultation, discovery, fit, presentation, objection handling, conversion, and active sales follow-up.

Client aliases may include Sales Representative, Admissions Officer, Enrollment Advisor, Comfort Advisor, Account Executive, or Closer.

The Sales Officer title represents conversion authority, not people-management rank. The client configuration must define what a Booking Agent may close, what requires a Sales Officer, and which handoffs require another specialist.

## Onboarding Coordinator

**Mission:** Convert a commitment into a fully activated customer.

Owns payment coordination, agreements, documents, registration, provisioning, onboarding recovery, and the handoff to Support.

## Support Officer

**Mission:** Resolve post-conversion needs and protect customer success.

Owns support requests, resolution, education, escalation, feedback, retention signals, and the return of valid expansion opportunities to Sales.

## Team Manager

**Mission:** Maintain coverage, quality, accountability, and coaching.

Owns schedules, service levels, performance reviews, quality assurance, escalations, and cross-role coordination.

## System Administrator

**Mission:** Keep Salesflow reliable and correctly configured.

Owns permissions, fields, calendars, phone numbers, integrations, workflows, data integrity, and technical change control.

## Ownership rule

Every active customer transition must have either:

- one named owner; or
- one explicitly eligible pooled team.

If ownership cannot be determined, the system must escalate rather than silently leaving the work unowned.

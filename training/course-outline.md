# Salesflow Booking Engine Certification

## Purpose

The course teaches the operating system. The embedded Knowledge Center provides the reference while employees work.

## Core certification

### Module 1 — Customer Journey

- Campaign → Capture → Convert → Close
- Standard, Assisted, Expedited, and Recovery Paths
- required, conditional, and skippable milestones

### Module 2 — Four Work Priorities

- Appointments
- Live Calls
- Assigned Tasks
- Call Queue
- interruption and availability rules

### Module 3 — Salesflow Records

- contact versus opportunity
- appointment versus task
- disposition versus note
- Call Brief versus historical context

### Module 4 — Calls and Handoffs

- inbound calls
- Zero Friction
- warm transfers
- failed transfer → book the correct appointment before ending the call
- one active call objective

### Module 5 — Documentation and Recovery

- completing a call record
- meaningful notes
- pipeline truth
- selecting the correct Recovery

## Role certification

After the core modules, assign the relevant role track:

- Booking Agent;
- Sales Officer;
- Onboarding Coordinator;
- Support Representative;
- Campaign Manager;
- Team Manager;
- Salesflow Administrator.

Each role track includes:

1. role mission and authority;
2. start-of-shift demonstration;
3. required click-by-click Salesflow Instructions;
4. scenario decisions across all four path tiers;
5. documentation exercise;
6. escalation exercise;
7. scored practical assessment.

## Passing standard

Certification requires:

- 80% or higher on knowledge questions;
- 100% accuracy on compliance and sensitive-data questions;
- successful completion of the role's practical scenario;
- correct disposition, opportunity update, next object, and handoff.

## Recertification

Require a short update lesson when a material workflow, interface, authority rule, or compliance requirement changes. Do not require full recertification for formatting-only documentation changes.

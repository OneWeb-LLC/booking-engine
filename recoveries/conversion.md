# Conversion Recovery

Use when a qualified sales conversation occurs without commitment.

## Triggers

- unresolved objection;
- proposal or estimate pending;
- another decision-maker is required;
- payment decision pending;
- customer requests follow-up;
- opportunity stalls inside the active sales window.

## Default owner

The Sales Officer who owns the relationship, unless the client explicitly uses a pooled follow-up team.

## Execution choice

- exact-time promise → Appointment;
- personal action → Assigned Task;
- pooled outreach → Call Queue;
- customer returns live → Live Call.

When the active sales window expires, transition eligible contacts to Reactivation rather than leaving them indefinitely active.

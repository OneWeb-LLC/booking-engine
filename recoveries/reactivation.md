# Reactivation

Use when an eligible lead or customer is outside the active follow-up window and no current commitment is open.

## Eligible sources

- dormant leads;
- closed-lost opportunities that remain eligible;
- expired proposals or estimates;
- previous customers;
- manually approved reactivation segments.

## Guardrails

Exclude active opportunities, upcoming appointments, current conversations, DND contacts, invalid numbers, recent outreach, and conflicting workflows.

Reactivation should be capacity-paced so it fills available Call Queue inventory without displacing fresh demand.

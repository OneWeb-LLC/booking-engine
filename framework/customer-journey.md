# Customer Journey and Golden Path

## Golden Path

The ideal Booking Engine journey is:

1. Lead Generated
2. Lead Activated
3. Contact Established
4. Fit and Intent Qualified
5. Booking or Live Handoff Secured
6. Appointment Completed
7. Customer Converted
8. Payment Completed
9. Customer Onboarded
10. Customer Supported

Not every client requires every stage, and ready customers should not be forced through unnecessary steps.

## Journey matrix

| Stage | Ideal outcome | Default owner | Broken transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead Generation | Valid demand enters Salesflow | Campaign Manager | Lead Processing Recovery |
| Activation | Immediate contact attempt | Booking Agent/System | Activation Recovery |
| Contact | Meaningful conversation | Booking Agent | Contact Recovery |
| Qualification | Intent and fit established | Booking Agent | Qualification Recovery |
| Booking | Appointment or live handoff secured | Booking Agent | Booking Recovery |
| Appointment | Qualified conversation completed | Sales Representative | Appointment Recovery |
| Conversion | Commitment secured | Sales Representative | Conversion Recovery |
| Payment | Payment or approved plan completed | Sales Rep/Onboarding | Payment Recovery |
| Onboarding | Customer activated | Onboarding Coordinator | Onboarding Recovery |
| Customer Success | Need fulfilled and relationship protected | Support Representative | Support/Retention Recovery |
| Dormancy | Relationship remains active | Campaign Manager/Sales | Reactivation |

## Strongest-next-step rule

The Golden Path is guidance, not bureaucracy. If a qualified customer can convert immediately, do not create an unnecessary appointment. If preparation or another decision-maker is required, schedule the correct commitment.

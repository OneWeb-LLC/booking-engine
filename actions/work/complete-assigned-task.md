# Action: Complete an Assigned Task

## Outcome

A due personal responsibility is performed by its owner and converted into the correct customer and system state.

## Trigger and owner

Trigger: the task becomes due or overdue.  
Owner: the user named on the task.

## Rules

- Complete the promised action before completing the task.
- Do not use a task for exact-time appointments, pooled call work, historical context, or pipeline state.
- Create a new object only when new work genuinely exists.

## Completion and failure

Complete only after the promised action and required record updates are finished. If blocked, retain ownership and escalate with the blocker, due impact, and requested decision.

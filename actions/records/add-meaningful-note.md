# Action: Preserve Meaningful Context

## Outcome

Important internal context is preserved without burying the record in automated-event noise.

## Trigger and owner

Trigger: an interaction creates durable context another human may need.  
Owner: the user who learned or confirmed the information.

## Rules

- Record motivation, objections, preferences, decision-makers, commitments, and unusual circumstances.
- Do not use a note for due work, exact-time commitments, pipeline state, or routine automated events.
- Never store unnecessary sensitive information.

## Completion and failure

Complete when concise, factual, searchable context is saved on the correct record. Remove or escalate accidental sensitive-data exposure according to client policy.

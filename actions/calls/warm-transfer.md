# Action: Warm-Transfer a Live Customer

## Outcome

The customer and receiving person are connected with sufficient context, and responsibility is explicitly accepted.

## Trigger and owner

Trigger: a live customer requires another role or greater authority.  
Owner: the transferring user until the recipient accepts.

## Rules

- Explain the transfer to the customer.
- Brief the recipient before connecting.
- Confirm both parties can hear each other.
- Remain responsible until the recipient accepts.
- Recover the live customer immediately when the transfer fails.

## Completion and failure

Complete only when the recipient accepts and the live connection is confirmed. If the transfer fails, return to the customer and select the strongest remaining live action.

# Action: Start a Shift

## Outcome

The user is available for the correct work, prepared for scheduled commitments, aware of due responsibilities, and able to reach required handoff coverage.

## Trigger and owner

Trigger: the user's scheduled shift begins.  
Owner: every active Salesflow user.

## Completion standard

- correct client account;
- accurate availability;
- functioning calling equipment;
- next appointment reviewed;
- due work identified;
- transfer and escalation coverage known.

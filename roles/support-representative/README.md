# Support Representative

## Mission

Resolve post-conversion needs, protect customer success, and route valid commercial opportunities back to Sales without abandoning the support issue.

## Start of shift

1. Confirm availability and support-channel coverage.
2. Review scheduled support appointments.
3. Review due and overdue Assigned Tasks.
4. Review escalations and aging unresolved cases.

## Core work

- answer inbound support requests;
- identify the exact issue and desired resolution;
- resolve within authority or create an accepted escalation;
- document durable context;
- protect the customer from being pushed into active sales workflows;
- route valid expansion interest to a Sales Officer.

## Path behavior

Customer Journey path tiers primarily govern conversion work. Support uses the same operating discipline: resolve the standard request, assist when extra help is required, expedite urgent valid issues, and recover broken service commitments.

## Guardrail

Do not transfer a customer merely to remove the issue from your workspace. Responsibility moves only when the receiving person or team accepts the handoff with context.

## Scorecard

- first-response time;
- resolution time;
- first-contact resolution;
- escalation acceptance;
- reopened issues;
- documentation quality;
- customer retention signals.

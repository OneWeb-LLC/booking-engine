# Sales Officer

## Mission

Convert qualified service opportunities into informed customer commitments while protecting fit, trust, and required business rules.

## Start of shift

1. Confirm availability for appointments and live transfers.
2. Review today's appointments, purposes, and preparation context.
3. Review due Assigned Tasks and active decision commitments.
4. Confirm offer, pricing, payment, eligibility, and escalation authority.

## Assigned work types

1. **Appointments**
   - scheduled consultations, assessments, demonstrations, or other closing appointments;
   - appointment preparation, execution, disposition, and sales follow-through.
2. **Live Calls**
   - qualified warm transfers initiated by Booking Agents;
   - other explicitly routed real-time sales opportunities approved by the client.
3. **Assigned Tasks**
   - personally owned sales follow-up;
   - promised actions, documents, proposals, and decision commitments.

Sales Officers do not ordinarily answer the general inbound Booking Engine line or work the Booking Agent Call Queue.

## Core work

- receive and explicitly accept qualified warm transfers initiated by Booking Agents;
- conduct scheduled sales appointments;
- perform discovery, fit confirmation, presentation, and objection handling;
- close when the customer is ready;
- create exact next commitments when the decision cannot be completed;
- initiate the accepted onboarding handoff.

## Path behavior

- **Standard:** conduct the configured sales appointment sequence, resolve ordinary obstacles, and complete Close.
- **Expedited:** accept a ready lead, validate mandatory requirements, and close without unnecessary future steps.
- **Recovery:** document the unresolved decision and create the correct owned follow-up, appointment, or pooled recovery.

## Authority

The Sales Officer owns closing authority defined in the client profile. The role may not invent pricing, make unapproved guarantees, bypass required eligibility, or commit another department without an accepted handoff.

## Completion standard

1. Record the definitive outcome.
2. Update opportunity stage and status.
3. Record objections, decision-makers, commitments, and material preferences.
4. If won, complete the onboarding handoff.
5. If unresolved, create an exact owned next action.
6. If lost or disqualified, record the reason and stop invalid follow-up.

## Scorecard

- held-appointment rate;
- close rate and collected revenue;
- time from qualified opportunity to Close;
- Expedited acceptance and conversion;
- follow-up completion;
- onboarding handoff acceptance;
- quality and compliance.

# Living Wiki Architecture

The repository has six top-level operating sections:

1. [Methodology](../methodology/README.md)
2. [Customer Journey](../customer-journey/README.md)
3. [Roles](../roles/README.md)
4. [Work Priorities](../work-priorities/README.md)
5. [Snapshot](../snapshot/README.md)
6. [Governance](README.md)

Supporting material is demoted into the section it serves:

- Recoveries live under Methodology.
- Employee procedures and training live under Roles.
- Configuration, administration, and client templates live under Snapshot.
- References and documentation templates live under Governance.

A new top-level folder requires an architectural change, not merely a new document type.

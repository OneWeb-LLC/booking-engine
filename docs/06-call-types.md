# Universal Call Taxonomy

Call purpose, trigger, priority, and execution method must remain separate fields.

## Inbound

- Sales or Booking
- Support
- General Business
- Return Call
- Escalation

## Outbound

### 1. Lead Activation

- new paid-ad lead;
- new website lead;
- new referral;
- new chat or form inquiry.

Preferred execution: Zero Friction when eligible, followed by Call Queue recovery.

### 2. Engagement Response

- missed inbound call;
- explicit call request;
- qualified high-intent click;
- conversation response;
- approved abandonment signal.

### 3. Appointment Management

- confirmation;
- reminder;
- reschedule;
- cancellation recovery;
- no-show recovery.

### 4. Booking Recovery

- new lead failed to book;
- Zero Friction failed to connect;
- qualification ended without booking;
- booking flow was abandoned.

### 5. Active Follow-Up

- lead failed to progress;
- proposal, estimate, payment, or decision pending;
- follow-up requested;
- open opportunity inside the configured active window.

### 6. Reactivation

- opportunity older than the active window;
- closed-lost but eligible;
- dormant customer;
- expired estimate;
- approved reactivation segment.

## Configurable windows

Do not hard-code 30 days universally. Each client must define its active follow-up window and reactivation threshold.

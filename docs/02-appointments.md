# Appointments

## Definition

An appointment is a scheduled commitment requiring attendance at a defined time. Exact-time callbacks are appointments.

## Required appointment data

- appointment type;
- contact;
- owner or eligible team;
- start time and time zone;
- purpose;
- preparation context;
- location, meeting link, or phone method;
- desired outcome.

## Operating standard

1. Review the next appointment before its preparation window.
2. Read the latest Call Brief, task, and meaningful notes.
3. Begin on time.
4. Move the contact to the strongest valid next step.
5. Record the disposition and pipeline movement.
6. Schedule any exact-time commitment before ending.

## Appointment outcomes

- completed and progressed;
- completed with follow-up required;
- rescheduled;
- canceled;
- no-show;
- disqualified or closed;
- escalated.

## No-show recovery

A no-show must immediately exit the appointment workflow and enter the configured recovery path. Recovery may include an SMS, a call, a new Call Queue entry, and a rescheduling link.

## Guardrail

Do not force unnecessary appointments. If a qualified person can complete the desired action now, preserve live momentum.
